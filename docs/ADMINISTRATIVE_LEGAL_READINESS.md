## Evidence discipline

Every item starts as **NOT VERIFIED**. A repository change or checked Markdown box is not evidence of external completion. Use **EVIDENCE REVIEWED** only after an authorized human records the narrow fact supported, a non-sensitive evidence locator, the reviewer and role, the review date, and any expiry or review trigger.

Sensitive records stay outside Git. Do not place government identifiers, bank details, contracts, legal advice, insurance documents, personal contact details, penetration-test reports, university correspondence, credentials, or account numbers in this file.

Allowed status language is:

- **NOT VERIFIED** — sufficient evidence has not been reviewed.
- **HUMAN-ONLY — ACTION REQUIRED** — an authorized person or external professional must act or decide.
- **WAITING ON THIRD PARTY** — an authorized request exists but its outcome is not verified.
- **EVIDENCE REVIEWED** — a human reviewed evidence for one narrowly stated fact; this is not a general compliance claim.
- **NOT APPLICABLE — EVIDENCE REVIEWED** — an authorized professional documented why an item does not apply and defined when it must be reconsidered.

## Responsibility handoff

The owner must explicitly assign and obtain acceptance for business/corporate administration, tax/accounting, privacy, security, and institutional/procurement responsibilities. Each assignment needs a primary and backup, a restricted contact locator, an accepted date, and a review trigger. One person may hold multiple roles, but a published inbox alone is not evidence that a responsibility is staffed.

- [ ] **HUMAN-ONLY — ACTION REQUIRED:** the owner and an appropriate Ontario legal/registration professional identify the actual operating structure and determine whether business-name registration, incorporation, extra-provincial registration, or another registration is required.
- [ ] Record only the narrow reviewed conclusion, jurisdictions, effective or expiry dates, renewal owner, and evidence IDs.

## 2. Corporate records and annual filings

- [ ] Determine whether an entity exists and which corporate-record, beneficial-ownership, address, director/officer, annual-return, renewal, and other obligations apply.
- [ ] If applicable, establish a restricted records location, deadline calendar, responsible person, backup, and professional escalation path.

## 3. Banking and accounting readiness

- [ ] The owner, accountant, and financial institution determine whether and when a separate business account is appropriate and what documentation is required.
- [ ] Define bookkeeping categories, receipt retention, reconciliation cadence, spending authority, reimbursement process, and year-end handoff for the confirmed structure.

## 4. GST/HST and tax-accounting handoff

- [ ] Do not encode a tax threshold in this checklist as a substitute for current professional advice.

## Review cadence and external-action stop

A human owner should schedule at least an annual review, or another cadence required by the relevant professional. Review sooner after revenue, marketing email, personnel or contractor, entity or jurisdiction, data-use or provider, institutional-proposal, or material security/privacy changes.

For each review, record the review date, reviewer and role, scope and evidence IDs, decisions changed, unresolved human-only actions with owners and targets, and the next review date. No such review is recorded by this repository today.

Before any filing, application, contract acceptance, insurance purchase, marketing send, penetration test, certification engagement, or University communication, an authorized human must separately confirm that their identity and authority are documented; the entity, jurisdiction, recipient, scope, and current terms are verified; required professional review is complete; ongoing costs and obligations have owners and backups; no unsupported compliance, certification, insurance, affiliation, or approval claim will be made; and sensitive evidence will remain outside Git.

Every external-action item remains **NOT VERIFIED**. Repository changes alone cannot satisfy this checklist.
