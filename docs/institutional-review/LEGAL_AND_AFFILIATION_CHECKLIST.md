# DPA, security-addendum, trademark, and affiliation checklist

> **DRAFT — HUMAN/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED. THIS IS NOT LEGAL ADVICE, AN AGREEMENT, OR EVIDENCE OF UNIVERSITY APPROVAL.**

## DPA/security-addendum intake

- [ ] Confirm the contracting party, legal status, signing authority, and institutional counterparty; nothing in this repository establishes them.
- [ ] Define service scope, roles, data categories/subjects, purposes, instructions, and prohibited uses from the approved trust/data inventory.
- [ ] Review privacy/security obligations, applicable law, cross-border terms, subprocessor approval/notice, confidentiality, return/deletion, audit, assistance, incident notice, termination, and liability language with qualified counsel.
- [ ] Validate requested security schedules against current evidence. Reject or qualify unsupported uptime, response-time, certification, audit, residency, recovery, penetration-test, and insurance representations.
- [ ] Decide whether institutional data is permitted at all and whether proposed use triggers new PIA/security review or product/architecture changes.
- [ ] Record negotiation owner, exceptions, approvals, effective/renewal dates, and continuing obligations in an access-controlled system.

## University trademark and affiliation

- [ ] Confirm the current written relationship, if any, with an authorized University of Toronto/UTM representative. Default: **no relationship or endorsement is claimed**.
- [ ] Obtain appropriate legal review of product name, domain, screenshots, building/campus references, metadata, marketing, app-store content, and institutional materials.
- [ ] Do not use university crests, official wordmarks, seals, endorsement language, or confusing presentation without written authorization.
- [ ] Keep the independent-service statement clear and proximate in reviewer/public materials.
- [ ] Confirm source and permission for every third-party logo, mark, map, photograph, and campus asset used in a handoff.
- [ ] Record written permissions, restrictions, expiry, attribution, and owner outside the public repository where appropriate.
- [ ] Re-review after any partnership, procurement, naming, branding, or domain change.
