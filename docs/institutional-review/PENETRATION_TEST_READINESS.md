# Independent penetration-test readiness checklist

> **PLACEHOLDER — NO PENETRATION TEST HAS BEEN PERFORMED OR SCHEDULED BY THIS DOCUMENT**

Do not describe an assessment as independent unless the assessor is demonstrably independent of implementation and explicitly authorized by the system owner.

## Before engagement

- [ ] An authorized owner approves purpose, budget, assessor, and timing.
- [ ] Owner and counsel review contract, confidentiality, data handling, liability, safe-testing, and reporting terms.
- [ ] Scope lists exact authorized domains, APIs, auth roles, repositories, environments, exclusions, and third-party or provider constraints.
- [ ] Rules of engagement prohibit social engineering, denial of service, destructive actions, access to real student data, and provider testing unless separately authorized.
- [ ] Synthetic test accounts and data are prepared; production-testing and emergency-stop conditions are explicitly decided.
- [ ] Named operational contact, escalation channel, window, source addresses, and evidence-transfer method are confirmed outside this repository.
- [ ] Assessor methodology, qualifications, independence, and conflicts are documented.

## Deliverables and closure

- [ ] The report identifies scope, dates, methods, limitations, severity method, evidence, findings, and reproducibility guidance.
- [ ] The sensitive report is stored access-controlled; a separately approved summary is prepared if needed.
- [ ] Every finding has an owner, disposition, target date, and validation evidence.
- [ ] Risk acceptances are time-bounded and owner-approved.
- [ ] Retest scope and results are documented by the independent assessor.
- [ ] Public or institutional wording is approved and states limitations.
