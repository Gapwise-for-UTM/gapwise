_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

These terms govern use of Gapwise for UTM, including the optional Gapwise AI integration. Gapwise is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the University of Toronto.

## 1. What Gapwise provides

Gapwise provides timetable parsing, gap planning, campus-navigation information, optional private cloud features, friend-overlap features, and optional AI-integration tools. Some information is computed from user-supplied timetable data and public or reviewed campus data.

Gapwise is not an official academic record, accessibility service, emergency-navigation service, or university scheduling system. If Gapwise conflicts with an official University of Toronto source, the official source controls.

## 2. Accounts and optional features

Core guest functionality may be available without an account. Features such as private sync, friend overlap, and private AI delegation may require authentication.

You are responsible for protecting access to your account/device and for promptly revoking integrations you no longer want to use.

## 3. AI integrations

AI integration is optional. Connecting an AI provider does not itself grant timetable access; supported private data categories and write permissions require separate Gapwise delegation.

When you authorize and invoke an external AI provider, that provider receives the specific Gapwise tool output necessary for the request and processes it under its own terms, privacy policy, account settings, and retention practices.

Academic/ACORN-imported meetings are read-only through Gapwise AI. Supported AI writes are limited to explicitly permitted personal planning/preferences and remain subject to Gapwise validation.

## 4. Routing, timing and recommendations

Campus routes, transition estimates, accessibility states, gap recommendations, leave-by times, and similar results are planning aids. Gapwise deliberately exposes uncertainty and may report data as approximate, inferred, unknown, or unavailable.

Do not rely on Gapwise as a guarantee that a route is open, accessible, safe, or sufficient for a time-critical situation. Verify important accessibility, building-access, emergency, examination, academic, and safety information through official sources when necessary.

Good-faith security research must follow the published Security Policy.
